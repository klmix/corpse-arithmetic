    IV

    Verdict

    Rules modelled: side initiative, melee on Strength and ranged on Presence, Agility defence, crits and fumbles, armor tiers and their Agility penalty, shields, Omens, the Broken table, hemorrhage, Morale, and creature specials such as extra attacks, fear, ambush, paralysis, drain and lingering curses.

    Not modelled: Powers and scrolls, fleeing player characters, healing during the fight, ammunition, and most class abilities beyond DR modifiers. Anyone who hemorrhages survives only if the party wins and can treat them.
